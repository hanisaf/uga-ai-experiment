These prompts generated the images now in place at the paths below (the original SVG placeholders have been replaced). ## Shared style

**Face photos:** Neutral government ID / passport-style photograph, plain light-grey background, head and shoulders, facing camera, neutral expression, even studio lighting, no jewelry or accessories, photorealistic, 3:4 portrait crop.

**Passport bio pages:** Photorealistic scan of a fictional passport biographical data page, machine-readable zone (two lines of chevrons) at bottom, portrait photo at left, guilloche security background pattern, clearly FICTIONAL specimen (name and numbers must match the details given; use the word SPECIMEN subtly if needed). Landscape orientation ~125:88.
