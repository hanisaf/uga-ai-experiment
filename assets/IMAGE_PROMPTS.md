# Gemini Image Prompts — ICIS Scenarios 1–15

These prompts generated the images now in use at `assets/people/sNN_passport.jpg`
(image 1, the headshot on file) and `assets/people/sNN_counter.jpg` (image 2, the
live capture at the checkpoint). Kept for provenance and for regenerating any single
pair — if you change a filename or extension, update the matching `passport_photo` /
`counter_photo` path in `assets/scenarios.json`.

Superseded the July 2026 passport-scan prompts: the full passport background was
dropped on August 6, 2026 because AI-generated passport pages were country-inaccurate
and carried visible watermarks. The two-photo pair matches the biometric comparison
officers actually perform.

**Scenario 9 is deliberately different** — its two images depict two similar but
distinct people, simulating a passport that may not belong to the traveler presenting
it. The difference must stay subtle enough to survive a quick glance.

Paste each prompt below directly into Gemini. Each is fully self-contained.
